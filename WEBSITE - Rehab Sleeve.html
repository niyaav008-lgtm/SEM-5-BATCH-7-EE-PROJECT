What data does the app expect from the ESP32?

Bluetooth (Nordic UART service). One reading per line, up to 10 times a second, in this order:

fsr,flex,ax,ay,az,servo
2048,1900,0.02,-0.01,1.00,45

fsr and flex: 0 to 100, or raw 0 to 4095. ax, ay, az: acceleration in g (m/s² also works). servo: servo position or assistance, 0 to 100 %. JSON also works: {"fsr":..,"flex":..,"ax":..,"ay":..,"az":..,"servo":..}
